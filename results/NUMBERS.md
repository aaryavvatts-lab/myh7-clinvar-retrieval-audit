## Conclusions that CHANGE (honest reading; nothing tuned to preserve v5)

1. **The v5 cohort was truncated.** 906 of 3,184 ClinVar missense variants (28.5%) were never retrieved (MyVariant stores no or another gene symbol for them; ClinVar's own GeneSymbol list contains MYH7). Residues 763-893 and 1549-1770 had zero ClinVar variants in v5 (267 and 349 exist). v5 was 71.5% of the full missense set; 78 v5 'missense' records are not in the new set (66 were stop-gains), 15 v5 SNV ids no longer exist in the current release.
2. **v5 labels were also too permissive.** v5 called a variant pathogenic if any single condition record was P/LP. ClinVar's variant-level aggregate calls 219 of those Conflicting/VUS. Using ClinVar's aggregate, P falls from 511 to 392 and benign from 110 to 30; of v5's 72 frequency stand-ins, 58 are ClinVar VUS and 6 ClinVar pathogenic (they were only benign because ClinVar records were missing). Both effects are reported separately in the sensitivity cohorts.
3. **Permutation test: v5 rejected; v6 primary does NOT** (observed 0.4815 on 304 scoreable variants of which only 7 are benign; unconditional null p=0.567; coverage/burial-stratified null p=0.181). Power is very low (7 benign), so this is 'no evidence', not 'evidence of no signal'. With the v5 label rule on the full data the test rejects again (p=1e-4 under both nulls) but the observed AUROC is 0.705 (v5: 0.842), so completing the data alone removes most of the headline score performance.
4. **Coverage asymmetry persists under ClinVar-aggregate labels and is larger**: no labelled neighbour for 76.7% of benign vs 24.2% of pathogenic; coverage flag AUROC 0.762 (v5 0.650). Under the v5 label rule on complete data it shrinks (flag AUROC 0.562, OR 3.41 vs v5 12.8). So the existence of asymmetry is robust, its size is not: it depends on how benign/pathogenic are defined. Residue-clustered inference widens the OR interval to [1.71, 97.88] (naive Fisher p=1e-17 in v5 is not valid).
5. **AlphaMissense head to head: v5 'no measurable edge' no longer holds.** On scoreable variants 3D-PPS minus AlphaMissense = -0.431 (CI excludes 0); the covered-subset comparison has 1 benign variant and is untestable.
6. **Coverage-matched strata are uninformative** (benign counts 23 / 6 / 1 / 0 across neighbour groups); 3D-PPS within strata is 0.483 (1 to 2) and 0.49 (3 to 5).
7. **3D vs 1D**: paired difference includes zero in the primary cohort (+0.063 [-0.084, +0.151], 7 benign); the v5 claim that 3D structure adds anything over a sequence-distance control is untested and unsupported.
8. **Models**: adding the coverage count alone (no score value) gives the same gain as adding 3D-PPS (A_3d minus A_cov: +0.001 wider, +0.010 tighter, equivalent within 0.05 by TOST). Dropping ClinVar-trained predictors costs about 0.07 AUROC (CI excludes 0), so v5's 'barely changed' is false. Calibration slope is 0.54 (balanced) / 0.77 (unweighted): over-confident, not 'well calibrated'.
9. **Hotspots**: the v5 hotspot (L120F, F121L; V123A no longer passes) is still found, plus M90V/M90R/M92V; the screen shrinks from 6 to 5 with the floor instead of 22 to 3 (denser labels mean fewer single-neighbour artefacts).
10. **Spatial block hold-out (new)**: 3D-PPS generalises no better than chance to held-out 100-residue blocks in the primary cohort (0.49, 3 scoreable benign) while AlphaMissense stays 0.90; with the v5 rule (55 benign) 3D-PPS is 0.925 on only 9 scoreable benign (CI not reliable) and paired difference vs AM +0.05 [-0.13, +0.24]. The result is therefore not a stable property of the score.
11. **Previously empty regions** are now covered by 616 ClinVar missense variants; see the regions table below. They change the picture mainly by adding pathogenic and VUS residues to the lever arm and rod, and by showing that most benign evidence remains outside them.
